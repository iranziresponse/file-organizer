What Orch is actually for

Less searching around, more getting back to the work

Orch is not trying to replace your laptop habits. It sits beside them and helps when downloads, class work, dates, and projects start spreading everywhere.

Why Orch

The normal mess of PDFs, Word files, screenshots, WhatsApp files, class notes, and forgotten deadlines.

What it helps with

A few useful places instead of one crowded Downloads folder

Orch works best when you let it handle the repetitive parts, then step in when a file or date needs your judgment.

Files that arrive every day

Notes, assignments, past papers, reports, slides, images, and zipped project files can be sorted into places that make sense.

Unsure files wait

If Orch is not confident, it does not pretend. The file waits in a review area so you can choose where it belongs.

Deadlines and classes

MUELE dates, class timetables, exams, and your own added dates can sit closer to the files they belong to.

Revision that starts from your files

Sorted notes can feed topic lists, weak areas, past paper review, summaries, and study reminders when you want them.

Projects and career proof

Keep project notes, screenshots, reports, and links together so finished work is easier to explain later.

Optional connections

Use MUELE or Google Drive if they help you. Leave them alone if you only want local sorting on your laptop.

Under the hood

Good things to know before you install

Orch is made to be useful without asking you to trust a mystery box. You can see what it moved, change your mind, and choose which extras you want.

It keeps a record

When Orch moves a file, it keeps a simple history so you can search recent moves or undo a mistake.

It asks when unsure

Files that look private, unclear, or hard to place wait for your review instead of being moved silently.

You can start without extras

MUELE, Google Drive, summaries, and other helpers are there when useful. Basic sorting can still be simple.

It is open to feedback

Questions, bugs, missing course details, or ideas can be sent from the support popup. The address is filled in for you when your email app opens.

Contact support

Start small

Try Orch with the work you already have.

Point it at a folder you trust, then let it help with the next files that land on your laptop. You can start with school work, a personal project, or a messy Downloads folder.
