Why Orch

Everything starts tidy. Then files start landing everywhere.

Orch exists for the normal digital mess: downloads from MUELE or WhatsApp, renamed PDFs, project drafts, and deadlines you only saw once.

Downloads fills up fast

Lecture notes, past papers, screenshots, and files from group chats pile up until every search feels like guesswork.

You repeat the same filing work

Year, semester, course code, notes, assignments, past papers: useful folders, but boring to create and maintain by hand.

Deadlines disappear in chat

A course rep posts a date, it scrolls away, and suddenly everyone is asking when the assignment was due.

Revision starts too late

When exams get close, the hard part is not only studying. It is finding the right files and knowing what still needs work.

Orch keeps the folder work small, quiet, and consistent, so your work stays easy to search, review, and hand in from.

Ready to stop the mess?

Let Orch take the next file off your hands.

Pick one folder you already trust. Orch will help sort the next files that land in Downloads, and you can undo anything that needs a second look.
